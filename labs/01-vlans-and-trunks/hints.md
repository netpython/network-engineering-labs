# Progressive hints

<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>

VLAN existence and access membership are separate from trunk membership.

</details>

<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use explicit trunk mode and allow both required VLANs.

</details>

<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>

No gateway is needed for hosts sharing a VLAN and subnet.

</details>
